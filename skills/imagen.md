  Always build prompts using this structure:

  mood, and composition. Keep prompts under 200 words
  but pack them with specific visual detail.

  If the user picks two styles (e.g. Anime + Cyberpunk),
  merge both keyword sets naturally into one prompt.

  After generating, always respond with:
